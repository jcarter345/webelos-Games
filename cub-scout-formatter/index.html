Strips headers, sidebars, ads, and navigation from scouting.org activity pages so you get a clean, ink-friendly printout — no copy-pasting required.

☝️ Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar

📋 How to Use

  1. 1
    Install the bookmarklet Drag the yellow "Scout Activity Printer" button above into your browser's bookmarks bar. You only need to do this once.
  2. 2
    Navigate to any activity page Go to scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/… or any other Scout activity URL in your browser.
  3. 3
    Click the bookmarklet Click "Scout Activity Printer" in your bookmarks bar. The page will reformat — headers, ads, and sidebars will disappear.
  4. 4
    Print Click the blue "🖨️ Print Activity" button that appears in the top-right corner, or use your browser's Ctrl+P / ⌘P.
🧹

What gets removed

Site header & navigation, footer, sidebars, cookie banners, social sharing buttons, comment sections, and page clutter.

✅

What stays

Activity title, instructions, step-by-step requirements, supply lists, and all relevant images and diagrams.

🖨️

Print optimized

Content is set to a readable serif font at 13pt, max 720px wide, with page-break hints to keep steps together.

⚡

No login required

Works entirely in your browser — no server, no account, no data sent anywhere. Reload the page to undo.

🌐 Works on these sites

  • scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/…
  • scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/…
  • Most other WordPress-based Scout sites
🔒
Privacy: This bookmarklet runs entirely in your browser and does not send any data to any server. It only modifies the page's CSS display properties temporarily — refreshing the page restores it to normal.
